The Golden Cup and Handle Pattern According to the world's greatest investing
education website, a cup and handle is a technical chart pattern that represents a cup and handle
where the cup is in the shape of a U and the handle has a slight downward drift. First described by
the market technician and Jedi, William J. O'Neill, in his 1988 classic book on technical analysis,
How to Make Money in Stocks, a cup and handle is considered a bullish signal
extending an uptrend and is used to spot opportunities to go long. O'Neill teaches
us to look for these characteristics. Length generally cups with longer and more U-shaped
bottoms provide a stronger signal. Avoid cups with a sharp V-bottom. Depth ideally the cups
should not be overly deep. Avoid handles that are overly deep also as handles should form the top
half of the cup pattern. And volume. Volume should decrease as prices decline and remain lower than
average in the base of the bowl. It should then increase when the stock begins to make its move
higher back up to test the previous high. And as bespoke has been pointing out all year,
Gold prices have been forming a decade-long cup and handle formation,
