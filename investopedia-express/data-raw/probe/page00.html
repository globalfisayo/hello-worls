Talking Points

Recent interactions between the hosts and their guests.

The U.S. Economy on the Precipice
Q: What is the current underlying strength of the U.S. economy beyond the headline numbers?
Market-readiness and the AI-driven wealth effects are masking softer real growth; earnings have remained strong, but job creation is uneven, inflation sticks, and sentiment remains depressed, suggesting fragile footing despite surface strength.
Guacamole, Money and Markets
Q: Tell us your origin story. How did you get to do what you do today?
In high school I was in a magnet program and initially thought I'd be a mechanical engineer, then shifted to business. A paid internship at Scott Trade opened my eyes to online brokerages and the retail investing wave, setting me on a path to finance and financial education.
The Risk v. Reward Paradox
Q: What should a young investor do to start building a portfolio?
Start with ultra-diversified defaults like target-date funds, then learn and adjust gradually; focus on asset allocation, automate contributions and rebalancing, and avoid chasing perfection by staying in the market over the long run.
The Risk v. Reward Paradox
Q: What does risk mean to you and how do you define it?
Risk is a mix of quantitative and qualitative factors, but for most investors it comes down to understanding your personal risk profile and time horizon, guiding how you allocate assets rather than trying to perfectly time the market.
Crypto’s Next Chapter
Q: What are some practical use cases of crypto for small businesses today?
Small businesses use blockchain to track inventory, accept crypto payments at checkout to reach younger shoppers, and pay employees in crypto, which can reduce intermediaries and fees while increasing speed and ownership.
